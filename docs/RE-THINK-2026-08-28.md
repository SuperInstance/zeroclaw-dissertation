## 1. Penrose aperiodic tabling as the embedding substrate

**The math.** Penrose tilings (P2: kite/dart; P3: rhombi with angles multiples of 36°) tile the plane aperiodically: local matching rules force global non-repetition [V — standard result, Gardner/Conway literature]. Two properties matter to us:

- **Local rules → global order.** Two tiles either fit or don't (vertex/edge matching). A datapoint's neighbors are constrained by geometry, not by a learned similarity threshold.
- **Self-similarity at ratio φ.** Deflation (subdivide each tile into smaller tiles) and inflation (compose up) regenerate the tiling at scale φ [V]. The same object exists at multiple resolutions *exactly* — multiscale analysis without smoothing kernels.

**The embedding move [S].** Map each tile to a vector slot in a d-dimensional embedding: `tile_id → (center_position, tile_type, orientation_class)`. Adjacent tiles are *forced* to be correlated (matching rules), distant tiles are *statistically* decorrelated but never independent (long-range order via Ammann lines — the induced line sets that carry the tiling's hidden 5-fold order [V]). This is what a learned embedding wants to be — **locality-preserving, no collision (aperiodicity = no exact repeats), built-in multiscale (φ-deflation)** — but obtained by construction instead of gradient descent. Concretely: an n-token sequence maps to an n-tile patch of P3 rhombi; token i lands on tile i; token-token relations become tile adjacency / Ammann-line co-membership / deflation-parent relations. These are **discrete, exactly computable relation matrices** — no floating-point similarity to launder.

**The material-science hook [V].** Penrose tilings *are* the mathematical model of quasicrystals — Shechtman's 1982 discovery, Nobel Chemistry 2011. Quasicrystals give: pure-point diffraction (structure without periodicity — order that survives Fourier analysis), and **phasons** — collective rearrangement modes that shift the tiling's phase without destroying local order. Phason is exactly the right name for what the elephant registers: a room's mood shifting *as a whole* while every local dial-pair relation stays intact. The μ̂/κ machinery (elephant `vmf.py`) currently measures direction and concentration of a dial cloud; on a tiling, κ-like rigidity becomes *phason stiffness* — how much coordinated shift the local matching rules absorb before the pattern tears.

**Post-quantum ideation [S→M].** Casey's phrase. The defensible core: quasicrystal embeddings are **non-periodic but long-range-ordered**, and their natural algebraic description is a **cut-and-project from a 5-d (P2/P3: Z⁵-ish) lattice** — embeddings that live in higher dimensions and are *viewed* through a 2-d aperiodic window [V — the standard model-theoretic construction]. For reasoning substrates this offers something periodic lattices and hash-space can't: **no aliasing at any scale** (aperiodicity defeats periodic-collision attacks, including Shor-style structure exploitation), while retaining exact algebraic structure (the projection lattice). Whether this constitutes a robust "post-quantum" substrate is [S] — the claim that matters and is testable: *aperiodic relation structures resist the collision/periodicity exploits that break structured embeddings.* That is a registrable experiment, not a slogan.

## 2. Origin-centric cellular thinking + Pythagorean snapping

**The math [V as math; S as dissertation tooling].** Fix an origin O. A position is *snap-valid* iff ‖p − O‖ ∈ ℚ exactly — concretely, p lies on a circle of Pythagorean-triple radius (3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, …, parameterized by Euclid's formula (m²−n², 2mn, m²+n²)). Snap every cell to the nearest valid radius (and angle grid). Consequences:

- **All distances from origin are rational** → finite, exact, comparable without floating-point tolerance. Metric bookkeeping becomes integer/rational arithmetic (the same honesty discipline the journal hash-chain gives events).
- **Connections-gravities-weights baked into the coordinate system:** promote the metric from Euclidean to a field-weighted metric d_w(p,q) = d(p,q)·g(p)·g(q) where g is a per-cell gravity read from the dial vector. The metric *is* the field [M→S]. This is a discrete cellular realization of the elephant's warmth/κ: κ high (cold, rigid) = gravity smooth, paths short and stiff; κ low (warm, loose) = gravity rough, many ways to be — the same monotone the vMF spec already guards ("warmth reads μ̂ only; κ reads ρ only"). Here the separation is enforced by construction: gravity modifies the metric, warmth modifies a direction field over it, and the two enter different operators.
- The tit-quilt-elixir cells become the point particles: each cell's journal trajectory (`{cell_id, ts, opcode, dials_before, dials_after}`) is a **walk on snapped coordinates**, and restart/tear entries become metric discontinuities — the walk-integrity measurement the STATUS doc already flagged as genuinely new.

## 3. Permutation group theory + set theory for tensors

**The math [V].** Tensors with index symmetry are representations of the symmetric group S_n; Schur–Weyl duality decomposes (ℂ^d)^{⊗n} under S_n × GL(d) via Young diagrams [V — classical]. Two direct payoffs:

- **Permutation-equivariant tensor algebra:** a room-field snapshot over k cells must be invariant/equivariant under cell relabeling (cells are processes; their order is arrival order, not identity). DeepSets/set-transformer results formalize this: equivariant maps are exactly the structure-preserving ones [V]. The elephant's aggregation (pool dial vectors per tick) is already a symmetric map — the theory tells us what the *complete* class of legal aggregators is, and what the current pool discards.
- **Set-theoretic foundations for walks [S]:** define a walk as a *set* of (tile-edge, weight, ts) triples, not a sequence. Sequences impose total order; sets carry exactly the order structure earned by timestamps. Dissertation walks on speech timelines and fabric journal-walks become the same object: **edge-sets on a Penrose patch**, with S_n acting on edge labels. Edge-set equivalence classes = walks up to relabeling — the natural grain for cross-strata transfer (Tap corpus ↔ fabric corpus), which is already an open thesis item.

## 6. Library blueprints (build order)

### Lib 1 — `penrose-quilt` (geometry substrate) — **build first**
- **Does:** generate P3 (rhombi) patches by deflation from a seed; exact adjacency, Ammann-line co-membership, and deflation-parent relation matrices over tiles; tile→slot indexing; φ-rescale of any patch.
- **API:** `patch = Patch.inflate(seed, n_levels)`; `A, L, D = patch.relations()` (adjacency, Ammann, parent — sparse boolean matrices); `patch.map_tokens(tokens) -> Assignment`; `patch.deflate(k)`.
- **Consumers:** dissertation (edge-sets substrate), elephant (embedding layout for sensor fusion), tit-quilt-elixir (a cell-placement mode — later, via export).
- **Failure mode:** relation matrices grow O(n·φ²) per deflation level — level cap + sparse-only storage; floating-point vertex drift in deflation — use exact algebraic coordinates in ℚ[√5] (φ = (1+√5)/2 is algebraic; all vertices live in ℚ[√5], so exact arithmetic is available and mandatory [V]).

### Lib 2 — `pythag-snap` (coordinate/metric module)
- **Does:** origin-centric coordinate assignment with Pythagorean-radius snapping; gravity-weighted metric d_w; rational-exact distance bookkeeping; discontinuity (tear) markers.
- **API:** `p = Snap.position(angle_idx, triple=(m,n))`; `g = Snap.gravity(dial_vector)`; `D = Snap.metric_matrix(cells, g)`; `Snap.tear(before, after) -> bool`.
- **Consumers:** dissertation (walk metric), fabric journal export (tear annotation).
- **Failure mode:** triple lattice is sparse (radii grow ~quadratically) — angle resolution must snap too, and snapping *is* a modeling choice that must be registered like any threshold, else it launders structure into the metric.

### Lib 3 — `perm-tensor` (symmetry toolkit)
- **Does:** permutation-equivariant aggregators beyond pooling (Young-symmetrized projections); edge-set walk objects with relabeling quotients; equivariance tests (the guard the vMF spec's rotation-invariance argument wants, generalized).
- **API:** `W = Walk(edge_set)`; `W.canonical()` (relabeling quotient); `T = SymTensor(data, partition)`; `assert SymTensor.equivariant(fn)`.
- **Consumers:** elephant (legal aggregator class), dissertation (cross-strata transfer grain).
- **Failure mode:** canonicalization cost (graph-isomorphism-hard in the worst case) — cap n, fall back to invariant checksums with disclosed incompleteness.

Lib 1 is implementable from this blueprint alone; Libs 2–3 depend only on Lib 1's `Patch` interface. Cross-section/heat-head mapping rides any plotting layer and is not a separate library.

**Honest bottom line:** the aperiodic-substrate payoff (exact, learned-free relation structure) is real and buildable [V-adjacent]; whether it *improves measurement* over the current dial-space instruments is [S] and must be answered by the same pre-registered apparatus — the re-think expands the laboratory, it does not relax its rules.
