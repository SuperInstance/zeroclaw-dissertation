## 1. How this reads from outside

Stripped of its own vocabulary, this project is doing three things at once.

**First, it is a metrology of a social Markov blanket.** The "room-field" is what my field would call an observable blanket statistic: the room is the statistical boundary structure through which a set of agents couple, and the elephant is a low-dimensional summary of the ensemble's internal state — a von Mises–Fisher mean direction μ̂ (a shared prior over how to be) and a concentration κ (the precision of that prior). Their slogan "cold room = one way to be, warm room = many ways to be" is, in predictive-processing terms, exactly "high-precision prior vs. flat prior." The acclimation curve (exponential relaxation of an agent embedding toward the room) is belief updating with a fixed Kalman gain; charisma pull is the action half of the active-inference loop — the agent perturbing the blanket rather than just reading it. None of this is a criticism. It is a competent re-derivation of the standard ensemble picture, with the unusual and genuine advantage that here the blanket statistics are *directly instrumented* rather than inferred from behavior.

**Second, "Walks, Not Waves" is a position in an old navigation debate.** They have sided with path integration over cartography: the primary object is the odometric log (edge sequences reconstructed from speech events, now emitted natively by the fabric journal), and the field snapshot is a derived readout, not the territory. The RE-THINK then does something navigation theory will recognize instantly: it installs a *fixed, non-learned prior geometry* — an aperiodic tiling with an exact multiscale deflation ladder (ratio φ) — as the scaffold on which representations live by construction rather than by gradient descent. That is structurally a grid-module architecture with a lattice prior: multiscale, exact, similarity-free. The phason vocabulary (collective mode that shifts the phase without tearing local order) is correctly imported from quasicrystal physics, and credit is due — they named that loan themselves.

**Third — and this is what an outsider notices first — the epistemic apparatus is running far ahead of the phenomenon.** The claim ledger, as reported in STATUS: every phenomenon-side hypothesis (premise, H-GEN, reader-delta, switch test) has returned VOID, INDETERMINATE, or FALSIFIED. What survives as SOLID — thermometer, edge log — is instrument-side: properties of the apparatus. The method chapter ("six launderings caught") is openly presented as the durable contribution, and the honesty markers [V]/[S]/[M] read like a lab that has been burned and built scar tissue. This is either admirable discipline or a slow-motion discovery that the phenomena are not load-bearing, and the document culture does not yet distinguish those two readings. Meanwhile the geometry foundation is exact, tested, and measures nothing yet — a laboratory built before the assay. The one body of material that resembles naturalistic observation is the field-note corpus of moment-absence (five nights, "the shadow of a moment"), which is eloquent and entirely unmeasured; it is quietly the most interesting data in the repo and the least theorized.

## 2. Three imports that could sharpen the room-field formalism

**(a) Error-gated learning rate — Pearce–Hall attention / precision-weighted prediction error.**
*Concept:* In animal learning, the update rate is not fixed; Pearce–Hall (1980) established that attention, and hence learning rate, is gated by unsigned prediction error (surprise). The predictive-processing generalization is precision-weighting of prediction errors (Feldman & Friston).
*Analog:* `acclimation_curve` uses a fixed rate (their `rate` = the agent's modulation skill, 1/τ). Skill-only acclimation says a newcomer warms to a room at a speed that depends on the newcomer; error-gating says it also depends on the entry surprise |agent − room| and on the room's κ (how costly non-conformity is there).
*Testable consequence:* On the existing Tap newcomer entries (displacement 0.830, κ 21→47, D′ half-life ≈ 20 msgs — already in the ledger), pre-register a rivalry: fixed-rate exponential vs. error-gated (Pearce–Hall) relaxation. The gated model predicts half-life stratifies with entry warmth gap and entry κ — e.g., entrants into high-κ rooms sharing the room's warmth converge faster than entrants who share warmth but enter a loose room. A single global half-life across entry conditions falsifies the gated model; clean separation supports it. Uses existing corpus; one number decides.

**(b) Rate remapping vs. global remapping (hippocampal remapping literature).**
*Concept:* Hippocampal maps reorganize in two qualitatively distinct modes: *rate remapping* (firing rates change; the relational map stays intact — Leutgeb et al. 2005) and *global remapping* (the whole relational map is redrawn). The distinction is measurable and behaviorally meaningful.
*Analog:* Their phason/tear pair is this distinction under another name: a phason shift is μ̂ rotating (and κ drifting) while local dial-pair relation structure is preserved; a tear is relation-structure change. The import makes "the room turned" measurable: a witnessed turn should be a global remap, not a phason.
*Testable consequence:* Define a two-part transition statistic over consecutive field snapshots — (i) μ̂/κ change with relation matrix preserved, (ii) relation-matrix change — and register the prediction that the moments corpus is *bimodal* on it: real turns are tears, non-turns are phasons. If transitions are unimodal, or turns co-occur with phason statistics, the phason/tear vocabulary is decorative. The same statistic applied to fabric journal walks gives tear-annotation a falsifiable grain: journal-annotated tears should coincide with global-remap statistics, never phason statistics.

**(c) Grid-module scale-ratio theory (entorhinal multiscale codes).**
*Concept:* Entorhinal grid cells come in modules whose scales form a geometric progression; measured inter-module ratios cluster near 1.4 (Stensola et al., Nature 2012), and theoretical work (Mathis and colleagues) derives optimal ratios in roughly the 1.3–2 band depending on the coding objective. The point of the theory: the scale ratio is a *tuned parameter with a derivable optimum*, not a gift of the substrate.
*Analog:* The φ-deflation ladder (same patch exactly at scales 1, ψ, ψ², …) is structurally a grid-module ladder with ratio φ ≈ 1.618 — squarely inside the theoretically motivated band, a coincidence the RE-THINK never notes. The import: stop treating φ as free structure; treat it as a candidate-optimal ratio for a specified statistic.
*Testable consequence:* Cross-strata transfer (open Q4) should be *scale-selective*: walks comparable across strata only at matched (or theory-predicted pairs of) deflation levels; transfer as a function of level mismatch should peak and decay. Flat transfer across level pairings means the multiscale substrate is aesthetic, not functional. Sharper still: the optimal ladder for a dispersion statistic (κ) need not equal the optimal ladder for a direction statistic (warmth) — two ladders, two registered tests.

## 3. Reinvented wheels (with their existing names)

1. **The "diffusion gravity field" is diffusion maps / Laplacian eigenmaps** (Coifman–Lafon; Belkin–Niyogi), and in navigation terms a **successor representation** (Dayan 1993; Stachenfeld et al. 2017's "predictive map"). Lazy neighbor-averaging for k rounds is a truncated Neumann series of the lazy random-walk operator — a polynomial spectral filter. The digit-for-digit verified "strictly decreasing variance" is the heat equation's maximum principle, not an empirical finding. If the field is meant to be a "gravity," the SR literature already says what a graph-diffusion field *means* behaviorally: a value function, with the round count as the horizon (discount).

2. **The "construction-null clause" is a graph-Laplacian-regularized initialization plus a configuration-model null.** The locality-correlated embedding is one step of feature/label propagation (Zhu–Ghahramani lineage); the honest null for "was the similarity structure earned?" is degree-preserving randomization of the graph (the configuration model, network science). Their clause says "compare against a locality-correlated-by-construction baseline of the same dimension" — right instinct, wrong null class; same dimension is not the control, matched degree/adjacency structure is.

3. **The vMF dial machinery is directional statistics and population-vector coding.** Mean resultant length ρ, concentration κ, the MLE equations — textbook (Mardia–Jupp; Batschelet); the population-vector reading of an ensemble's mean direction is the Georgopoulos lineage. Their independently derived √(N/d) small-sample bias of ρ is the classical small-sample bias of the resultant length, with published corrections (James-type shrinkage). The "warmth reads μ̂ only, κ reads ρ only, so neither can move the other" gate is the factorization of the vMF sufficient statistics — correct, and already a property of the estimator family, not a design achievement.

*Minor, for completeness:* Pythagorean snapping is lattice quantization (Gersho–Gray) with an unusual 1-D radial lattice of quadratically thinning density; fixed-rate acclimation is a first-order lag / Rescorla–Wagner curve; walks-up-to-relabeling is graph canonization and motif orbits (the nauty problem). None of this is disqualifying — reinvented wheels usually mean the road is real — but the dissertation should cite the existing names, because reviewers like me will find them anyway, and the current framing presents several as novel.
