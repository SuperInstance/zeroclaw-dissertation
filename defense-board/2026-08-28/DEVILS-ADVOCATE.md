## Attack 2 — Wave-4 is attempt 2 after repositioning, with an uncounted multiplicity structure

**The attack.** Wave-3's honest negative said α lives where the legs are blind. Wave-4 moves α into the fiber's within-night target trajectory — into the detector's field of view. The design change is disclosed, but two accounting problems are not on any checklist. First, **trials accounting**: a success on repositioned attempt 2 is strictly weaker evidence than first-pass success, and the freeze text contains no clause quantifying or even acknowledging the discount. Second, **multiplicity**: the success criterion is disjunctive — "P_trans (or a pre-named leg) separates α=0 vs α=1... OR gradient clause fires monotonically" — over a branch×leg matrix of at least ~20 cells (branches P, S, A, D, V, … × legs trans, rest, ratio, gradient, …). If per-cell false-fire probability under null is 1–5%, the probability that at least one cell "succeeds" by chance is 18–64% (1 − 0.95²⁰ = 0.64 at 5%; 1 − 0.99²⁰ = 0.18 at 1%). The tautology guard (α=0 and α=1 verdicts identical) does not catch one-directional chance separations. Compounding this: the failure threshold is itself a range — "inseparable within 1–2 orders of room-draw scatter" — which one is it? A failure criterion with a two-order-of-magnitude slack band is post-hoc-able. And the wobble carrier opens a leakage channel (legs reading per-t target-logging structure rather than α) that the VOID clause addresses only if the phase-lock check is specified as a statistic with a threshold, which it currently is not.

**Evidence.** S2-FREEZE-PREP Clause 2 (matrix dimensions, "etc." ellipses), Clause 4 (the disjunctive success, the "1–2 orders" failure band), §1 (RIVAL amplitude audit — unfalsifiability in either direction), the tautology guard text, five α-only matched pairs in n=16 corpora with power analysis still pending ("Block everything downstream until power is certified").

**Survival conditions.** (a) The empirical false-fire rate of the full verdict matrix on α=0/null data is measured and sits below the disjunction's resolution — measured, not asserted. (b) The failure threshold is pinned to one number before S3. (c) The freeze contains a trials-accounting sentence: "success after repositioning is attempt-2 evidence; the false-fire audit bounds its strength." (d) The wobble-laundering VOID clause is mechanical: name the statistic, the threshold, the trigger.

**Cheap decisive test.** False-fire audit, compute-only, finishes before S2 lock: run the complete branch×leg verdict matrix on existing wave-3 corpora at α=0-equivalent legs (fiber v4 at α=0 is v3-parity — byte-check parity first, do not assert it), with matched-pair room-draw scatter resampling to generate the null distribution of "any cell fires." If ≥1 cell fires under null at realistic scatter, the success disjunction must be tightened (pre-named primary endpoint, or ≥2 concordant cells required) before S3 generation spends the sealed corpora.

## Attack 4 — The geometry foundation's verified content is definitional, its "no window parameter" claim is false as stated, and its "exact arithmetic" is coarse quantization

**The attack.** Three prongs.

(a) **Tautology prong.** The two headline "verified" numbers are construction-true. Adjacent-pair cosine 0.7394 follows from a locality-initialized embedding — §1.5 says "by construction," and the only real finding there is the construction-null clause, which is a negative result about embeddings. Diffusion variance 1.0000 → 0.6894 strictly decreasing is the generic behavior of local averaging: on regular graphs it is provable (doubly-stochastic update, Jensen); on non-regular graphs it is generic though not universal. Strict monotonicity on the Penrose patch therefore carries essentially no information about Penrose structure. The §2.2 claim that this "supports apparatus generality" of the field machinery is confirmed trivially — it would have been a bug report if it had failed.

(b) **Window prong.** §2.1 claims "Tile adjacency carries no window parameter" — but §1.1 defines the patch as deflation "restricted to a fixed origin-centric window of radius 2φ^g." The window did not disappear; it moved from the edge log into patch generation, and it co-varies with generation level. Boundary-tile adjacency depends on the window radius, so a walk near the patch boundary has different relation structure than the same walk on a larger patch. Meanwhile the exact cross-scale relations (deflation-parent, Ammann co-membership) that would make multiscale comparison exact rather than window-contingent are unimplemented (§3 gap list) — and the deflation construction that would ground them was built and shelved. The two selling points over windowed approaches — "no window" and "exact multiscale" — are respectively false as stated and unbuilt.

(c) **Snap prong.** Pythagorean snapping is marketed as "exact distance bookkeeping... no floating-point tolerance," but the verified histogram shows raw distances 0.81–23.99 collapsing to {5, 13, 17, 25}. Under nearest-hypotenuse snapping, every distance below 9 lands on 5: the tile at raw distance 0.81 is recorded at distance 5 — a relative distortion of roughly +518%; two cells at true distances 9.5 and 13.5 (43% apart) get identical snapped distance 13; the verified examples 12.17→13 and 22.54→25 are ±7–11% distortions. Floating-point tolerance (~1e-15 relative) was never an artifact surface; snap quantization (double-digit percentages mid-range, catastrophic near origin) is one. The document half-concedes this ("snapping is a modeling choice that must be registered") while the headline still says exact. "Exact about labels, wrong about geometry" is quantization, not exactness — and it is being imported into walk metrics and tear-detection where those distortions become verdict inputs.

**Evidence.** GEOMETRY-FOUNDATION §1.1, §1.2, §1.4, §1.5, §2.1, §2.2, §3; the snap histogram {5:320, 13:535, 17:855, 25:530}; the shelved-deflation record; RE-THINK Lib 1/Lib 2 failure modes (which already contain half the fix).

**Survival conditions.** (a) §2.2's "supports apparatus generality" is demoted to "sanity check" unless a matched control fails to reproduce the monotonicity. (b) "No window parameter" is reworded to "window choice relocated to patch generation and registered as such," with demonstrated window-invariance of any walk statistic computed on patches. (c) "Exact" is struck from every sentence touching snapped distances, replaced with disclosed distortion bounds — or snapping is replaced by the exact ℚ[√5] vertex arithmetic already sitting in the library gap list, at which point snapping becomes purely a labeling scheme and the metric claim dies quietly.

**Cheap decisive test.** Half-day geometry audit script on quilt-geometry, three outputs: (i) run the same 8-round diffusion on an Erdős–Rényi graph matched to average degree 3.90, same seeding protocol — report the variance curve (prediction: strictly decreasing, same shape; if so, §2.2's support claim is struck); (ii) regenerate the gen-5 patch at window radii 1.9φ⁵ and 2.1φ⁵, remap a fixed corpus slice, recompute headline walk statistics — report verdict stability (if any verdict moves, the window artifact is alive and relocated, not eliminated); (iii) histogram of |snapped − raw|/raw over all tile-origin distances with max and 95th percentile (prediction: double-digit typical distortion; if so, the "exact" framing is struck).

## Attack 5 — The silence test cannot falsify the clock frame as registered, and it is built either-way-publishable

**The attack.** Four defects and one smell.

(a) **Granularity mismatch.** STEP=60 removes sessions containing ≥60 s silences. The clock frame at sub-60 s granularity — turn-taking rhythm, inter-moment spacing, pacing — survives untouched. A success verdict falsifies only the coarsest reading of the hermes objection; the strong form goes untested by design.

(b) **Selection confound.** The trimmed corpus is a different population (high-engagement sessions). κ persistence ≥90% and ICC ≥0.70 are both achievable through restriction of range and the trimming of noisy sessions regardless of whether clock or room is the generator. The registered success conditions read as validation but are compatible with pure selection artifacts.

(c) **The registration contradicts itself about its own effect size.** Same section: "~15–20% reduction from Tap corpus" and output "~400–480 records," versus "80–120 sessions remain from original ~500–600." Those are order-of-magnitude apart. If ~80–120 remain, the frozen rule removes ~80% of sessions — extreme selection, plus a real power question for ICC ≥0.70 at n≈100 with no registered power analysis. If ~400–480 remain, the intervention is mild and a κ-persistence success is nearly uninformative. A registration that cannot state what its own frozen rule does is not frozen; it is ambiguous, and the ambiguity sits exactly where post-hoc interpretation will later live.

(d) **The 60–90% dead zone.** κ persistence between 60% and 90% returns no verdict — the test has its own kill-band-shaped gap with no pre-stated tie-break, in a program whose recent history is landing in precisely such gaps (premise 0.5599; switch no-clean-win). The modal outcome may be another indeterminate, which the frame will file as progress.

(e) **Either-way-publishable.** "Either outcome is a methodological contribution; neither is a null result." When every outcome is publishable, the experiment risks nothing, and an experiment that risks nothing validates the lab's mood, not the world. Also: success condition 3 (ICC ≥0.70) partially rehabilitates the downgraded reader-delta construct through a side channel — a trimmed-corpus ICC win will be citable as reader-delta stability that the main line no longer claims.

**Evidence.** SILENCE-TEST registration text: corpus design section (the 15–20% vs 80–120 contradiction), thresholds table (60–90% gap, no tie-break), interpretation frame ("either outcome" sentence), deliverables (~400–480 records); standing context (reader-delta downgrade; the program's indeterminate-landing history).

**Survival conditions.** Amend before running — the test is non-blocking and pre-S3, so amendment costs wave-4 nothing: (i) add a sub-60 s permutation control as co-primary; (ii) reconcile the corpus-size contradiction with a one-page power analysis at the actual n; (iii) pre-state what κ persistence inside 60–90% does to the thermometer's SOLID status; (iv) reframe the "either outcome" sentence as a discipline statement about reporting, not an outcome valuation.

**Cheap decisive test.** Sub-60 s clock control on existing Tap data, compute-only, days: within-session permutation of inter-event intervals below 60 s (preserving dial values, event counts, and all ≥60 s structure), rerun the registered apparatus, report P_trans precision and κ on permuted vs original. Prediction (marked as such): if separation dies under fine-scale time permutation, fine-grained clock structure is load-bearing — and the registered silence test is structurally incapable of seeing the confound that matters.

The program should pre-register, before S2 lock, what that double-failure world means for the thesis (e.g., explicit pivot to method-and-apparatus contribution with all measurement claims suspended). Deciding that after S4, with the geometry frame already in place to absorb the blow, is how laundering number eight happens — and the RE-THINK has already pre-written its vocabulary.
