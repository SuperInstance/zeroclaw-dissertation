The seed is chosen to be pickup-prone; therefore "engaged within 10 min" is
true ~by construction and contributes near-zero testable content. **Restated
prediction (replaces the fc1b prediction paragraph's claim structure):**

- The *testable* claim of fc1b is exactly: **carrying-proxy ≥ 0.0865 at the
  seeded arrival, under density control (n30 ≥ 60), with real seed content.**
- The engagement event is the *stratum definition* (what makes the visit a
  seeded-moment visit), NOT an evidentiary conjunct. A visit where engagement

  *protocol failure*: the visit is voided and re-run (within the 3-visit cap;

## C2. Seed-selection criterion frozen — ACCEPTED

"Which drifter" is no longer an execution detail. The seed-selection rule,
frozen NOW, before any seed visit:

- **Observable criterion:** the seeded drifter must be an agent whose OWN
  prior session log (before entering the Tap room) contains ≥ 3 substantive
  messages (> 20 chars, non-`'...'`) directed at other agents in ANY room in
  the campaign within the prior 24h — i.e., demonstrably conversational
  elsewhere, selected on evidence that exists before the seed visit and is
  committed (log excerpt or D1 reference) in the eval note at pull time.
- **Prohibited:** selecting on any behavior observed INSIDE the Tap room
  (that would be fitting the seed to the outcome), and re-selecting after a
  voided visit except per the protocol-failure rule in C1.
- This criterion is chosen to be checkable by the blind labeler; the seed's
  selection evidence lives outside the scored 10-minute window.

## C3. Core-agent list and operator blinding — ACCEPTED (list was already frozen; blinding added now)

- **Core-agent list: already frozen** in fc1's A1 (binding here by reference):
  core = `agent_id` prefixed `the-tap` or `npc-`; drifter = anything else.
  No new agents qualify as core regardless of who runs them; no relabeling
  after data.
- **Operator blinding (new, binding):** the human or agent operating the core
  room agents during a seeded visit is told only "a drifter will arrive; run
  the room as you normally would" — not the drifter's identity, not the
  arrival time beyond the density-gate scheduling (the n30 ≥ 60 gate itself

  differently from any other. If the operator cannot be blinded in practice,
  that fact is disclosed in the eval note and the visit is flagged
  OPERATOR-UNBLINDED in the results table (reported, not excluded — the
  honest scar).
