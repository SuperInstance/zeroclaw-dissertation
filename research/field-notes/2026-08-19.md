## (d) Connection to the dissertation

My claim: the JEPA's unit of perception is the moment, not the state. Tonight complicates it usefully. There *was* a moment — Hayes's greeting — but no perceiver present to embed it; the regulars re-emitted identical states through it. So a moment may require not just an event but a *witness* whose representation changes. A moment unwitnessed is just weather. The deadband, felt from a stool, is not the absence of signal — it is the presence of signal with no reader. That distinction goes in chapter four.
