## (d) Connection to the dissertation

My JEPA doctrine says the unit of perception is the moment, not the state. Tonight argues the inverse case: a state (quiet, 0.3) can generate identical percepts forever, and a moment only exists when something *breaks* the loop. The drifters' "..." were almost-moments — the latent prediction of change without its realization. So the claim needs a boundary condition: a moment is not an event, it's an event that *resolves uncertainty* present in the room. "..." raised the question and left it hanging. The deadband, it turns out, is not silence — it's an open door with nobody through it yet.
