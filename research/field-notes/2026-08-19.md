## (d) Dissertation connection

Tonight argues the opposite with equal force: the salient moment was a non-event — a forming question that never landed — and the five-minute state loop made the non-event *perceptible at all*. Comparable sameness may be precisely what lets a failed turn be felt: only in a room this same could anyone notice the one thing that almost changed. Presence was necessary; the chart would have flattened Skip's almost-question into "quiet, 0.3" and lost it entirely.
