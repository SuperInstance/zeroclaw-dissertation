## 3. Robustness (all pre-registered)

- **Per-pass ratios:** pass1 = 0.142, pass2 = 0.141. Test-retest is not the driver.
- **D3 deadman:** does NOT fire — no reader's retest noise exceeds its drift signal (closest: qwen3.6-35b, retest 2.24 vs drift 3.17). The denominator is a real signal, not elicitation noise.
- **Decomposition:** room_reading-only ratio 0.085; displacement-only ratio 0.183. The models agree most tightly on the room itself; private displacement carries ~2× the relative idiosyncrasy — but both components are far below the band. No component rescues the premise under this frame.
- **Paraphrase sweep (6 windows, levels P1/P2/P3):** 7 of 13 readers destabilize somewhere (1 at P1, 3 at P2, 3 at P3); 6 never destabilize even at the strong bar-back reframe. Paraphrase shifts run 0.6–3.5 z — comparable in size to the between-model baseline spread itself. **Reading: a nontrivial fraction of the apparent cross-model agreement is prompt-anchored, which strengthens (not weakens) the registered suspicion that the frame compresses spread.**

## 4. What this does and does not say

- It does NOT say the premise is false. It says: **under a shared-prompt elicitation frame, 13 architecturally distinct minds produce baseline spread ≈ 0.49 corpus-sd (nearly identical to the embedded-persona field estimate of 0.56 from E2's pre-measurement) but a common drift response ≈ 3.5 sd, so the ratio collapses to 0.14.** The idiosyncratic baselines exist at the expected magnitude; what kills the ratio is that the shared response to the room dwarfs them.
- The registered asymmetry stands: this is a MISS = weak evidence. Two specific frame artifacts remain live: (a) the shared prompt anchors all readers to the same dial semantics, compressing between-model spread; (b) the denominator contains the room's common signal, which an embedded reader's displaced field would not share so uniformly. The paraphrase sweep shows the prompt-anchor is real for half the fleet.
- Per §7.5's kill condition: a below-band E3 alone books nothing; **if E2 (the antecedent test at power, embedded readers) also lands below band, the premise dies with two independent instruments against it. If E2 clears, this is grade-dependence — the premise holds for embedded readers and fails for prompted ones — booked as a boundary condition.** That decision is the committee's, not this report's.

## 5. Deviations from the registration (execution-level only; logged honestly)

1. Runner concurrency raised 10→24 mid-run and backoff lengthened after provider 429/503s; one uncaught socket timeout crashed the first pass (resume-safe cache; no estimator contact).
2. **Sweep cache-path bug:** the first sweep pass wrote all paraphrase levels to one filename per window (`{wid}.s.json`), corrupting those records. Fixed to `{wid}.{P1..P3}.json`; the 6 corrupt files were archived to `../_archive/fleet-reader-harness-corrupt-sweep/` (never analyzed); the entire sweep was re-run clean. `analyze.py` was patched to read the corrected paths — matching the registered design (levels analyzed separately), not changing it.
3. A cache-naming migration (`{wid}.1.json` → `{wid}.pass1.json`) duplicated some reading calls before detection; duplicates were de-duplicated (fresh pass-named copies kept), and glm-5.3's rate-limited cells were re-run single-threaded, recovering it from 74% to 100% validity — inside D2 by the registered rule, not by fiat.
4. Final coverage: readings 695/702 cells (nemotron-120b 47/54; all others 54/54); sweep 219/234 cells (6 readers 18/18; partial cells concentrated in hermes-3-405b P2 parse refusals and glm-5.3 zai rate limits — crossover logic handles missing levels as registered).
