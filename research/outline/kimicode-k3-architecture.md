## 0. My architectural philosophy, stated first

**A dissertation about walks must be a walk, not a snapshot.**

The dissertation's own doctrine — the doctrine it paid six launderings to learn — says the unit of
perception is the *edge* (field_before → field_after), not the snapshot; the wave vocabulary died on
2026-08-19 precisely because it described states where only transitions exist (`research/topic.md`, v1
post-mortem). My rival has built a *calibration certificate*: a document genre from metrology that an
instrument ships when it is **finished** — a snapshot of a settled state. That genre is false to the
material in three ways:

1. **The instrument is not finished.** The decisive experiment (E4 Clock-Split Stage 2) has not run; the
   rebound clock is open until 2026-09-18; the premise is registered INDETERMINATE. A certificate with
   pending items is a contradiction in genre — certificates do not have open edges. An edge log does:
   unclosed edges are a legal row type.
2. **The provenance is the evidence.** The discipline claim — the one thing `topic.md` calls "the durable
   contribution" — does not live in any number. It lives in the *time-series of verdicts*: six launderings
   caught, dated, in sequence, before filing. A verdict-first face page (the rival's Ch 1.3) flattens that
   time-series into a state and thereby hides the very data the payload claim is made of.
3. **The round's deepest result is about grain, and the architecture must enforce it.** The
   epistemologist's warning is absolute: conflating P1 (the 1.229 is length-confounded, condition grain)
   with P2 (the field is length, refuted at identity grain) is "the one epistemic error this round must
   not make" (`epistemologist-deepseek-pro.md` §1). An architecture that shelves the fired claim and the
   surviving claim in the same chapter re-creates the conflation risk at the structural level — a skimming
   committee reads one chapter as one tier.

So my architecture is **the edge log applied to the dissertation itself**, governed by four rules:

- **R1 — Grain-native shelving.** Every number is filed under exactly one grain — room-identity,
  room-condition, reader, or frame — and no claim crosses grains without a registered transfer instrument.
  Exactly one such instrument exists (ρ = +0.784, lensed-space only, conditional), and it gets the only
  cross-grain chapter in the book. Chapter headers name their grain. The silence test's fired/not-fired
  asymmetry becomes the filing system.
- **R2 — Self-application, with teeth.** The document opens with the claim ledger at *field_before* (the
  v2 state, before the silence test and the downgrade) and closes with the ledger at *field_after*; the
  walk between them is the dissertation. This is not decoration: any proposed section that cannot be
  stated as an edge (before → after, with a measured or registered delta) is definitionally ornament and
  is cut. R2 is the cut-the-crap test, mechanized.
- **R3 — No deleted numbers, only annotated ones.** Shared with the rival because the project rules
  (`topic.md`, Ch-7 rules) already impose it. The 1.229 appears everywhere it used to appear — with its
  decomposition annotation attached.
- **R4 — Branch-invariance.** The one decisive open experiment must not be able to collapse the
  architecture: every branch (PASS / KILL / INDETERMINATE) has a pre-stated home, and the title and spine
  survive all three. (The rival's R3, independently arrived at — the registrations impose it on any honest
  structure.)

The form that results: a prologue (field_before), three grain-parts, a payload part, an open-edge part,
and an epilogue (field_after) that computes the document's own delta. The dissertation is the one dataset
every reader can independently verify — running the instrument on it is the cheapest demonstration the
thesis can offer.

## 7. Why this structure wins (argued against the certificate)

1. **Genre truth.** The certificate certifies a finished instrument; this instrument has two open edges
   (Stage 2, the rebound clock) and a registered INDETERMINATE at its core. My rival handles this with a
   branch table (his R3) — which I adopt as R4 — but the branch table is an *escape hatch* bolted onto a
   genre that presumes closure. The edge-log genre has unclosed rows natively: the prologue is
   field_before, the epilogue is field_after, and an open edge is a legal state, not an embarrassment.
   A dissertation whose thesis is "walks, not waves" should not be filed as a snapshot.
2. **Grain discipline is enforced, not just stated.** The certificate's Ch 4 shelves the fired claim
   (1.229, condition grain) and the surviving claim (0.893, identity grain) two sections apart in one
   "Primary Readings" chapter. The epistemologist's entire note is a warning that these two must never

   committee cannot conflate what the table of contents keeps apart. The round's deepest result becomes
   the filing system — that is what it means for an architecture to agree with the evidence.
3. **The provenance is load-bearing, not decorative.** The discipline claim's evidence is the *sequence*
   of six dated launderings. The certificate flattens sequence into state (a verdict table on the face
   page, lineage retired to a retrospective). The edge-log keeps the time-series as the document's own
   skeleton: P.1 and E.1 are the same table at two times, and E.2 computes the delta. The dissertation
   thereby *runs its instrument on the one dataset every reader can verify* — the cheapest possible

4. **The cuts are sharper where the verdicts allow it.** Same verdicts, deeper cuts: narrative lineage
   → ledger rows; depth-sounder → paragraph; schema → appendix; moments → paragraph; Stage 1 correctly
   dated as finished. Nineteen recorded cuts against the rival's seventeen, with convergence honestly
