## TOP 5 TRANSFERABLE INSIGHTS

1. **The depth-sounder measurement principle (study-constraint-papers, COGNITIVE-CONSERVATION-LAW-v4).** The signal carries none of the information you want; the depth lives in the temporal gap, converted by an instrument that knows nothing about the water but everything about its own clock — and *changing the disc speed changes the resolution, not the phenomenon*. This is the reader-delta object stated exactly: the baseline is the spinning disc, the delta is the angular gap, and "what can we change?" (the disc) must be answered before "how do we measure it?"
