## FRONT 1: TAUTOLOGY/CIRCULARITY

**VERDICT: BREAKS-IF** — The definition has real content, but Clause 1 is dangerously circular.

**Analysis:**

- **Clause 1 (isotropy) is the danger.** "Stab_Aut(s) = Stab_{G_P}(s)" is built as a separating condition, yet the *definition of G_P itself* (P2) already requires "γ is a transition morphism + licensed by contract + preserves O_I." The "test" Clause 1 then performs is: *does the set of automorphisms we admitted into G_P exactly equal the automorphisms that actually fix s?* But G_P was built to be admissible; the test is verifying your group was well-defined, not checking general position. 

**Restatement risk:** If P2's definition of "licensed + transition morphism" is close enough to "fixes the orbit," then Clause 1 becomes "every orbit is fiber-respecting" — a restatement of what you *wanted* general position to ensure, not an independent condition that *earns* it.

**Repair:** Separate P2 into:
- G_P^{decl} = transition morphisms the contract *claims* are symmetries (no checkability burden)

Then Clause 1 genuinely tests: does the contract's claimed symmetry group match the verified one? (This is what you intend, but the phrasing obscures it.)
