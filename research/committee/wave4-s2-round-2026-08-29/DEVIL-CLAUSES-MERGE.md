### Clause 1: Phantom-scope clause
**Text:** "wave-4/4b numbers are instrument-calibration claims only; no field claims about real rooms, ever."
**Location:** Should appear in introduction or scope section of freeze draft
**Rationale:** Addresses fundamental objection that wave-4 is calibration on known-source phantom, with no claims about real-world rooms

### Clause 2: Retry-limit clause  
**Text:** "at most one further design iteration (4b); if it fails the gate, P is declared structurally unreadable for this engine and the instrument-vs-collapse program closes."
**Location:** Should appear in governance or iteration section of freeze draft
**Rationale:** Addresses structural temptation of unbounded retries; puts hard limit on design iterations

### Clause 3: Detector-independence clause
**Text:** "leg statistics consulted during design (P, at minimum) are labeled 'designed-for,' demoted from headline; the headline of any passing registration must be a leg untouched by the iteration (ICC decline or the V anchor leg), threshold re-derived fresh."
**Location:** Should appear in governance or analysis section of freeze draft
**Rationale:** Ensures headline results are not artifacts of iterative design process; untouched legs provide cleaner evidence
