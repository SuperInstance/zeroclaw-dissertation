0. PRELIMINARIES, STANDING ASSUMPTIONS, AND ILL-POSEDNESS REPAIRS

0.1 Objects. A room state is an empirical measure s = (1/N) Σ_i δ_{z_i} with
z_i ∈ S^6 ⊂ R^7, N ≥ 10 fixed. As a point set: s ∈ (S^6)^N / S_N (index
permutation quotient). I write Z(s) ∈ R^{N×7} for any window matrix realizing s.

0.2 Standing assumptions (stated because the prompt leaves them implicit):
 (A1) Dial frame. The ambient basis (mood, ..., presence) is FIXED and
      physically meaningful; ŵ ∈ S^6 is a fixed unit vector. No non-identity
      orthogonal map of R^7 is a semantic symmetry. Consequence: the tangent
      frame at μ̂ induced by the ambient dial axes is a legitimate frame in
      which C and A_1 carry absolute (not merely spectral) information.
 (A2) ρ > 0. μ̂ is undefined on the equatorial stratum {s : r̄ = 0}; all
      candidate observables H1–H4 are ill-posed there. I assume throughout
      that reachable states have ρ > 0. This stratum is nonempty and, note,
      is itself a fiber-collision artifact (any cloud with r̄ = 0 hashes
      identically to "undefined").
 (A3) Windows. The window sequence (z_1, ..., z_N) is a finite ordered sample;
      H1–H3 are defined on the unordered multiset, H4 additionally uses the
      given order.
 (A4) Dynamics (a)–(c) are as stated: linear acclimation toward the current
      room mean with per-agent rate β_j > 0, charisma pulls with blend factor
      (1 − e^{−γn}) ∈ (0, 0.15], and instantaneous finite rotations of the
      generating distribution's mean at exogenous switch times. Switch
      directions are exogenous and (this matters for Q6) I take "dynamics" to
      mean the closure under ALL admissible switch directions of bounded size,
      i.e. the reachability relation, not one fixed switch schedule.

0.4 Ill-posedness of the stated metric (adversarial repair). The prompt writes
‖s − s'‖_F for unordered multisets. The Frobenius norm is defined on N×7
matrices, i.e. on ORDERED tuples; for unordered states it is undefined until
one specifies an alignment. Repair (used throughout): the natural metric on
(S^6)^N/S_N is the optimal-matching (bottleneck-free) distance
    d(s, s') = min_{π ∈ S_N} ‖Z(s) − π Z(s')‖_F,
computable exactly by the Hungarian algorithm in O(N^3). Equivalently one may
use any L^p transportation metric on the empirical measures; all results below
are insensitive to this choice because every counterexample is stated with the
realizing permutation given explicitly. The bi-Lipschitz claims in Q6 must be
read in (d, ‖·‖ on the hash target); I use the ℓ∞ norm on concatenated hash
components. Any constant-Lipschitz statement is norm-equivalent up to a fixed
factor (all targets are finite-dimensional).

0.5 Two elementary lemmas used repeatedly.
 LEMMA 0.5.1 (polar decomposition of a cloud). Let μ̂ ∈ S^6, ρ > 0, s with
 mean direction μ̂. Every z_i decomposes uniquely as z_i = (μ̂·z_i) μ̂ + v_i,
 v_i ∈ T_{μ̂} := {v : μ̂·v = 0}, ‖z_i‖ = 1. Then Σ_i v_i = 0 iff r̄ ∥ μ̂
 (always true by definition of μ̂). Hence: the multiset of pairs
 (μ̂·z_i, v_i) determines s, and the tangent multiset {v_i} is balanced.
 LEMMA 0.5.2 (reflection through the axis). For μ̂ ∈ S^6 define the linear
 isometry σ_{μ̂} = 2 μ̂ μ̂^T − I ∈ O(7). It fixes μ̂ and negates T_{μ̂}.
 For z = cos α μ̂ + sin α v (α ∈ [0, π], v ∈ T, ‖v‖=1) it sends z to
 cos α μ̂ − sin α v: the axial coordinate is preserved, the tangent deviation
 is negated. σ_{μ̂} preserves r̄ exactly (linearity: Σ σz_i = 2(μ̂·Σz_i)μ̂ − Σz_i
 = 2Nρμ̂ − Nρμ̂ = Σz_i).

Q1. INHERITED BLINDNESS OF H1  —  PROVED (fiber statement), with the fiber
     STRICTLY LARGER than mean-neutral phase flips.

THEOREM Q1. Let h: (S^6)^N/S_N → R^7, s ↦ r̄ = (1/N)Σz_i. Then H1 = (μ̂, ρ)
is a Borel (indeed smooth on {ρ>0}) function of h alone, and:
 (i) [Invariance] For any perturbation δ = (δ_1, ..., δ_N), δ_i ∈ R^7, with
     z_i + δ_i ∈ S^6 for all i and Σ_i δ_i = 0, we have H1(s+δ) = H1(s).
     Hence every r̄-preserving perturbation is invisible to H1. PROOF:
     H1(s+δ) is a function of Σ(z_i+δ_i) = Σz_i. ∎
 (ii) [Fiber] The fiber H1^{-1}(μ̂, ρ) is the set of all N-multisets on S^6
     with resultant ρμ̂. On the open stratum where the points are distinct and
     ρ ∈ (0,1), this fiber is a smooth manifold of dimension 6N − 7 (6N point
     degrees of freedom, 7 independent constraints from the fixed resultant).
     For N ≥ 10 that is at least 53 dimensions of invisible freedom.
 (iii) [Fact 1 localization] "Mean-neutral phase flips" are precisely the
     perturbations of the window cloud (including temporal reordering and
     sign-symmetric reshaping) with Σδ_i = 0; hence mean-neutral flips ⊆
     fiber(H1). The inclusion is STRICT: e.g. tangent-deformation perturba-
     tions that move individual windows far from their originals while keeping
     the sum fixed are not "re-phasing" at all, yet lie in the same fiber.
     Fact 1's failure mode is thus exactly predicted by (i): the dead reader
     could not have distinguished anything inside one fiber, and re-phasing is
     only the empirically sampled slice of that fiber. The exclusion test in
     Fact 1 (r = 0.787 after removing mean-neutral flips) is consistent with
     the reader being a (noisy) function of H1 alone.

EXPLICIT FOOLING CLASS (F). For any e ∈ S^6, any u, w ∈ T_e ∩ S^6 (unit
tangent vectors), any θ ∈ (0, π/2): the two-window gadget
    z_± = cos θ · e ± sin θ · u
has resultant 2 cos θ · e independent of u. Take s = {z_+(u), z_−(u)} ⊎
{z_+(w), z_−(w)} ⊎ (N−4 copies of e) and s' the same with u, w replaced by
any rotated pair u', w'. Then H1(s) = H1(s') = (e, ((N−2) + 2cos θ... )) —
both clouds have resultant (N−2+2cosθ)e/... (concretely ρ = (N−2+2cosθ)/N).
The phase-flip subcase u' = −u is the pure "mean-neutral phase flip": the two
agents exchange above/below-mean identities. Note this gadget already exhibits
a room whose covariance differs wildly (u, w orthogonal vs u' = w) while H1
agrees — the fact that H1 also ignores second moments is a special case.

REFUTATION HALF. If "Fact 1's failure is exactly this fiber" is read as

Q4. NO BOUNDED-ORDER MOMENT LIST IS INJECTIVE; MINIMAL INJECTIVE OBSERVABLE

THEOREM Q4.1 (dimension obstruction; invariance of domain). Let X_N be the
top stratum of (S^6)^N/S_N (configurations of N distinct points), a smooth
6N-dimensional manifold, nonempty and open in its completion. If F: X_N → R^m
is continuous and injective, then m ≥ 6N. In particular every continuous
observable with target dimension m < 6N is non-injective. PROOF. Suppose m <
6N. Compose F with the inclusion ι: R^m → R^{6N} (last coordinates zero). Then
ι∘F: X_N → R^{6N} is continuous injective. Restrict to a small open ball
U ⊂ X_N ≅ R^{6N} (chart). By invariance of domain (Brouwer), a continuous
injective map R^{6N} ⊃ U → R^{6N} has open image; but (ι∘F)(U) ⊂ R^m × {0}
has empty interior in R^{6N}. Contradiction. ∎

COROLLARY Q4.2 (bounded-order failure). The degree-≤D polynomial moment list
has m(D) = dim{polynomials of degree ≤ D on R^7 restricted to the sphere} ≤
C(D+7,7) components, a constant depending only on D. For N > m(D)/6, m(D) <
6N, so by Theorem Q4.1 the moment list is non-injective on X_N. Since N is
unbounded and D fixed, no bounded-order moment hash is injective on rooms.
(This kills H1 (D=1, m=7), H2spec/H2full (D=2), and every fixed-degree
upgrade, all at once, for N ≥ 11 in the degree-2 case: m(2) = 28 < 66 = 6·11.
For N = 10, 6N = 60 < 64 = dimension of the H4 target — dimension alone does
not forbid injectivity of H4 at N = 10; it does for N ≥ 11. See Q5 for an
explicit structural failure valid at every N, which supersedes the count.)

THEOREM Q4.3 (bounded SUPPORT is what saves you: sharp moment order). Any two
N-atomic probability measures μ, ν supported on S^6 (or any subset of R^7)
that agree on all moments of degree ≤ 2N − 1 are equal. PROOF. Suppose μ ≠ ν.
Let supp μ ∪ supp ν consist of r + s ≤ 2N distinct points (r = |supp μ|,
s = |supp ν| ≤ N). Pick a generic linear functional λ: R^7 → R separating
these ≤ 2N points (exists: finitely many bad directions form a measure-zero
set). By univariate Lagrange interpolation there is a polynomial q of degree
≤ r + s − 1 ≤ 2N − 1 with q(λ x_i) = 1 on supp μ and q(λ y_j) = 0 on
supp ν ∖ supp μ (and q arbitrary on the latter). Set p = q∘λ, of degree ≤
2N − 1. Then ∫p dμ = 1 and ∫p dν = 0 for suitable endpoint choices (choose
q = 1 on supp μ, 0 on supp ν∖supp μ; if supp μ ∩ supp ν nonempty adjust the
values so the integrals differ, possible since the measures are not equal).
Then the degree-≤(2N−1) moments of p differ — contradiction with the moment
agreement. Hence μ = ν. ∎ (This is the multivariate Prony/interpolation bound;
equivalently: N-atomic measures are determined by moments through order 2N−1.)

SHARPNESS (counterexample at order 2N−2, on R; noted honestly as a
1-dimensional fact): for N = 2, μ = ½(δ_{−1} + δ_{+1}) and ν_b =
w δ_b + (1−w) δ_{−1/b} with w = 1/(1+b²), b ≠ 1, are distinct 2-atomic
measures agreeing on moments of degrees 0, 1, 2 (= 2N − 2): m_1 = 0 and
m_2 = 1 for both by direct algebra (wb² + (1−w)/b² = 1, wb − (1−w)/b = 0).
On the SPHERE the exact minimal order differs (the sphere's trigonometric
moment structure is more rigid: e.g. on a great circle, 2-atomic measures are
determined already at order 1 by unimodularity of atoms); the sharp spherical
minimal order between ⌈something⌉ and 2N−1 is left OPEN (see list), and does
not affect the conclusion: any FIXED order D fails for N > m(D)/6.

Q4.4 MINIMAL PROVABLY-INJECTIVE OBSERVABLE AND ITS COSTS.
    (a) The trivial hash H3 (the multiset, concretely: the N×7 matrix with
        rows sorted lexicographically in the fixed dial frame) is injective
        by construction. Costs:
        - DIMENSION: 7N reals parametrizing a 6N-dimensional set (the sorted
          locus is a union of N! closed polyhedral-manifold pieces glued along
          collision strata).
        - CONTINUITY: sorting is discontinuous exactly at collision states
          (two windows with equal dial vectors in the lexicographic order up
          to the truncation); these form a measure-zero but dynamically
          nonempty set (two agents can coincide). At a crossing, an
          O(ε) state change induces an O(1) ROW-SWAP change of the sorted
          matrix — though note the multiset itself (H3 as a measure) is
          continuous; only the sorted-matrix REPRESENTATION jumps.
        - FLOAT64 COLLISIONS: two distinct states whose sorted matrices
          differ by < 2^−52 ≈ 2.22e−16 per entry hash identically after
          rounding; and near-collisions (windows within ε) cause sort-order
          instability, with representational error O(ε) — bounded, harmless
          to the measure, but the "hash" output jumps. Conclusion: H3 is
          injective mathematically, ε-weakly-continuous, and 1e−16-dense
          collisions in float64. There is no cheaper continuous injective
          observable: by Theorem Q4.1 any continuous injective hash needs
          target dimension ≥ 6N, i.e. O(N) reals with constant ≥ 6 —
          "compressed" room hashes (H1: 7 reals; H2full: 28; H4: 64) are
          dimensionally forbidden from being injective for N ≥ 11.
    (b) Cheapest PROVABLE injective observable respecting the physics:
        moments through degree 2N − 1 (Theorem Q4.3), cost m(2N−1) ≈ (2N)^7/5040
        reals — cubic-to-septic in N, i.e. strictly worse than H3's 7N; its
        virtue is smoothness (polynomial in the points, no sorting), and its
        float64 conditioning degrades like cond(Vandermonde) — exponential in
        N in the worst case (near-coincident atoms), so it is not a practical
        hash either. Honest summary: injectivity costs O(N); every O(1)-size
        hash has a nontrivial fiber; the thesis question is therefore never
        "is the hash injective" but "is the fiber nontrivial ON THE REACHABLE
        SET" — which is Q6.

Q5. TEMPORAL BLINDNESS OF ALL ORDER-FREE OBSERVABLES; EXACT SURVIVORS OF A_1

THEOREM Q5.1 (order-free ⇒ rhythm-blind, tautologically). Let h be ANY map
defined on multisets (H1, H2spec, H2full, H3, and any F of the empirical
measure). Then for every ordered realization (z_1,...,z_N) and every π ∈ S_N,
h(z_1,...,z_N) = h(z_{π(1)},...,z_{π(N)}). PROOF. The value of h on a
sequence factors through its multiset by hypothesis; permutations do not
change the multiset. ∎  This is a definitional (not structural) blindness:
H3 is fully injective on multisets, so it "sees" everything order-free
observables can see; what NO order-free observable can see is the ORDER —
rhythm, phase, tempo, sequencing. Fact 1's "re-phasing" blindness can never
be fully repaired by an order-free upgrade (H2full included); the prompt is
right to add ordered statistics.

THEOREM Q5.2 (what A_1 closes, and exactly what it does not).
 (a) A_1 breaks the generic permutation. For a sequence with tangent
     deviations v_1..v_N in general position, the value Σ_{i=1}^{N−1}
     v_{π(i)} v_{π(i+1)}^T is maximized/unique to the identity-type orderings;
     random permutations change A_1 with high probability (the sum is a
     quadratic form in the permutation; its orbit under S_N has generically
     N!/2 distinct values). So H4 sees lag-1 rhythm GENERALLY.
 (b) EXACT SURVIVOR SET. Perm_{v}(A_1-preserving permutations) =
     {π ∈ S_N : Σ_{i=1}^{N−1} v_{π(i)} v_{π(i+1)}^T = Σ_{i=1}^{N−1} v_i v_{i+1}^T}.
     This subgroup (it is a subgroup: composition-stable, contains identity)
     always contains:
       (i) block-trivial permutations: π fixing the value sequence up to
           equality blocks (permuting within maximal blocks of equal v_i);
       (ii) collinear alternating classes: if all v_i ∈ {±a·u} ∪ {0} (collinear
           deviations) with sign sequence s_i, then A_1 = (Σ_i s_i s_{i+1}
           a²/(N−1)) uu^T depends only on the ADJACENT-SIGN-PRODUCT SEQUENCE
           (s_i s_{i+1})_{i=1..N−1} through its sum; every permutation
           preserving that sum survives. For the alternating sequence
           s = (+,−,+,−,...) (N even) EVERY adjacent product is −1, so EVERY
           permutation preserving antiphase structure survives, in particular
           the global phase flip s → −s.
       (iii) time reversal on symmetric-C clouds: π(i) = N+1−i maps
           A_1 = Σ v_i v_{i+1}^T to Σ v_{N+1−i} v_{N−i}... = A_1^T; whenever
           the realized A_1 is symmetric (e.g. collinear-tangent rooms, or
           any room with A_1 = A_1^T numerically), reversal is invisible to
           A_1 — and to every higher A_k (Σ v_i v_{i+k}^T → A_k^T): a room and
           its time-reversed twin have IDENTICAL lag-covariance spectra
           forever. This is the classical reversibility blind spot of
           second-order stationary analysis.
     Beyond permutations: by the dimension count of Q4 (H4 target has 7 + 21 +
     36 = 64 reals; ordered sequences have 6N), for N ≥ 11 the H4 fiber on
     ORDERED states has dimension ≥ 6N − 64 ≥ 2: the permutation survivors
     are a discrete shadow of a CONTINUOUS family of distinct rhythms with
     identical H4. H4 does not "close the permutation class"; it shrinks it

Q6.3 STRONGEST TRUE REPAIR (theorem). 
 DEFINITION. Let R(β, γ, S) ⊂ (S^6)^N/S_N-with-order be the reachable set

 exact-period phase shifts (E2); time reversal where it acts trivially (E3).

 THEOREM Q6.3 (conditional identifiability-modulo-symmetry, with explicit
 constant dependence). H4 is smooth and globally L-Lipschitz with
 L = ‖dH4‖_{∞,op} ≤ 4 (entries of C, A_1 are bounded quadratic forms on
 unit-vector rows; the gradients are bounded by 4 in ℓ∞-to-Frobenius norms;
 explicit computable constant, independent of dynamics). Moreover, for every
 ε > 0 and every compact K ⊂ R_ε (in the sequence topology, closure taken
 over admissible parameter families with switch count bounded), there exists
 c(ε, K) > 0 such that for all s, s' ∈ K with d(s, s') ≥ ε:
     ‖H4(s) − H4(s')‖ ≥ c(ε, K) · d(s, s').
 PROOF. The function (s, s') ↦ ‖H4(s) − H4(s')‖/d(s,s') is continuous and
 strictly positive on the compact set {(s,s') ∈ K×K : d(s,s') ≥ ε} (positivity
 is exactly the statement that no H4-collision pair lives in K×K, guaranteed

 non-G collision pair (s,s') existed in K, then by continuity of H4 nearby

 we excluded; for the residual zero-dimensional collisions, positivity on the
 compact set holds because the set of colliding pairs is closed and disjoint

 colliding pairs form a closed subset and its intersection with the compact
