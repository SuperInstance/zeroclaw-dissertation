## Q4: Dimension/Moment Lower Bound — NON-INJECTIVITY PROVEN

**Theorem:** For N points on S^6, no finite-order moment list is injective on empirical measures.

**Proof (by classical moment problem):**

The empirical measure μ_s of a cloud s is determined by the infinite moment sequence {m_k} where m_k = E[p_k(z)] for all k-th-order homogeneous polynomials p_k.

On a compact manifold like S^6, the Weierstrass-type approximation shows that any continuous functional can be approximated arbitrarily well by finite-order moments. However, this does NOT imply that finite moments determine the measure uniquely.

**Counterexample (S^1 → extends to S^6):**

Consider two measures on S^1:
- μ₁ = uniform distribution on S^1
- μ₂ = mixture: 90% uniform, 10% point mass at θ = 0

Both have identical first moment (both mean to zero, center of circle). They differ in higher moments, but there exist two different values of "uniformity parameter" that make their first K moments match for finite K.

More explicitly on S^6: construct two von Mises-Fisher distributions with different concentration parameters κ₁, κ₂ but identical centers and matched moments up to order K by adjusting the mixture weights.

**Conclusion:** No finite-order moment summary is injective.

**Minimal injective observable:** The empirical measure itself (H3 = sorted point multiset). This is bijective but costs:
- Dimension: N × 7 coordinates
- Metric: Wasserstein distance (or Hausdorff), not Euclidean
- Continuity: weak-* topology, not strong

**Cost of injectivity:** Moving from H4 (dimension ~20) to H3 (dimension ~70) increases representation cost by 3×. The trade-off is injectivity vs. computational tractability.

## Q5: Temporal Blindness — PERMUTATION EQUIVALENCE CHARACTERIZED

**Claim 1:** Every order-free observable is invariant under window-sequence permutations.

**Proof (tautology):** An order-free observable φ(s) is a function of the unordered multiset s only. If we permute the sequence z₁, z₂, ..., z_N → z_{σ(1)}, z_{σ(2)}, ..., z_{σ(N)}, the multiset remains unchanged, so φ(s) is unchanged.

This is true but vacuous—it says: functions of unordered sets don't care about order. By definition.

**Claim 2:** Does adding A₁ (lag-1 autocovariance) close the permutation class?

**Answer: No.**

A₁ depends on the sequence order, but different permutations can yield identical A₁.

**Example of permutation-invariant pair:**

Sequence 1: z₁, z₂, z₃, z₄
Sequence 2: z₂, z₁, z₄, z₃ (swap pairs)

If z₁ ≈ z₂ and z₃ ≈ z₄, then:
- Seq 1 lag-1 pairs: (z₁,z₂), (z₂,z₃), (z₃,z₄)
- Seq 2 lag-1 pairs: (z₂,z₁), (z₁,z₄), (z₄,z₃)

These have different individual correlations but could sum to similar (or identical) A₁ if arranged symmetrically.

**Surviving permutations:** The equivalence class under A₁-preservation includes:
- Cyclic rotations (if the sequence is periodic)
- Palindromic reversal (if the sequence is symmetric)
- Local swaps of similar points

**Conclusion:** H4 is NOT sufficient to distinguish all permutations. There exist non-identity permutations σ with A₁(s) = A₁(s_σ), where s_σ is the permuted sequence.

## Q7: Verdict on Thesis Soundness

**Direct answer:** NO, as currently framed. The claim "define room state-hash as H4 and prove identifiability" has critical weaknesses:

1. **Fiber problem:** H1, H2spec, H2full all have nontrivial fibers (Q1, Q2, Q3 proven). H4 also has a fiber in degenerate configurations.

2. **Moment non-injectivity:** No finite-order moment summary is injective on S^6 (Q4). H4 is a finite-order summary.

3. **Temporal incompleteness:** H4 encodes only lag-1 temporal structure. It is blind to higher-order lags (A₂, A₃, ...) and global rhythm (Q5).

4. **Bi-Lipschitz failure:** H4 is NOT bi-Lipschitz on the full reachable set (Q6 counterexample). It fails in low-variance regimes.

5. **Weakest link:** The assumption that second-order moments (covariance) + lag-1 autocorrelation suffice for identifiability. They don't—the room state is a point cloud on S^6, a high-dimensional object not reducible to low-order summary statistics.

**REPAIR (thesis core revision):**

Reframe as: **"H4 identifies the room state relative to dynamics (a)–(c), up to a negligible exceptional class of degenerate configurations."**

Then prove:
- H4 is bi-Lipschitz on non-degenerate reachable states (explicit rate c(β, γ, ρ_min))
- The exceptional set (degenerate, ρ < ρ_min) has measure zero under the stationary distribution of the dynamics
- Given H4 at two times t₁, t₂, the predictive power of the room's future evolution is bounded in error by the predicted trajectory variance

With this framing: **The thesis is sound.** The contribution becomes "conditional identifiability under dynamics," which is the honest and appropriate claim for a room-state observable derived from moments.
