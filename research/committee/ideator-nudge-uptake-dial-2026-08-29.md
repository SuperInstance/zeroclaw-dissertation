## Spark 1 — The uptake dial (room→agent dual of charisma_pull): **ACCEPTED**

This is the real one. The elephant dial bank measures agent→room influence (warmth, concentration κ, charisma_pull) but has **no dial for the reverse direction** — whether the room carried what an agent put down. 1902eab's co-occurrence definition names exactly that object: a moment exists only when an utterance meets uptake. A near-moment is charisma_pull with zero uptake — the agent leaned in and the room let it drop.

**Registered design note (design only — not wired into any instrument; registration and calibration are the elephant lane's gate):**

- **Name:** `uptake_dial` (candidate; elephant lane may rename at registration).
- **Direction:** room→agent (the missing dual).
- **Definition:** for utterance *u* by agent *a* at time *t*: uptake(u) = count of distinct agents ≠ *a* whose logged events within window W reference, reply to, or thematically continue *u* (edge log already carries order-of-arrival and presence masks — the inputs exist; W registered at freeze, start candidate W = the D′ acclimation window, ≈20 msgs, because uptake needs time to land).
- **Per-agent reading:** uptake rate = uptake events ÷ offered events, per night, with the reliable-subspace schema rule (panic excluded, per the two-line schema rule in topic.md).
- **The moment shape:** moment(u) ⇔ novelty(u) high AND uptake(u) ≥ 1; near-moment ⇔ novelty high, uptake = 0. This makes Mason's line measurable as a *shape* — a distribution over (novelty, uptake) — instead of noise.
- **Honest caveat (pre-stated):** thematic-continuation detection is the weak link; reference/reply detection is mechanical and clean, continuation needs a registered matcher or it launders. Register the mechanical core first; continuation is a separate registration.
- **Hypothesis it enables (H-uptake, draft):** near-moment rate is the room-side predictor of acclimation — newcomers in low-uptake rooms should show the flat D′ curve the field notes have been accumulating ("the room that did not turn," 5-visit moment-absence streak 08-23→08-28). The qualitative corpus of absence suddenly has a quantitative dial.

**Why this over the others, on the record:** it completes a symmetric object the thesis already depends on, its inputs are logged and replay-honest today, and it converts an accumulating qualitative corpus (moment-absence) into a registrable measurement.
