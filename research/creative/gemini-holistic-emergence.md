## The emergent claim

**The dissertation's object is the identity between the reader and the room — the hypothesis that a trusted reader is a room that has learned its own temperature — and the whole edifice is the instrument built to measure the slope of that identity.**

This is not the claim of any single piece. The room-field claims rooms have temperature; it never claims readers do. The reader-delta claims a reader drifts from a baseline; it never claims the baseline *is* a slow temperature. The premise claims baselines are reliable; it never claims they are reliable *in the same basis as the room's warmth*. Only the whole says it: that reader-identity and room-temperature share a home basis, and that whether this is *alignment* (the nurse is an instrument carrying information the room lacks) or *collapse* (the "trusted reader" is just room-warmth retrieval wearing a name) is a decidable quantity — the regress slope the convergence note registers. Slope ≈ 0 means the reader is a distinct instrument. Slope ≈ 1 means she was the room all along. The dissertation's real question is that number.
